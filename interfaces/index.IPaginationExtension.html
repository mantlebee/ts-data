    Interface IPaginationExtension<TItem>

    Extension that adds a Pagination to the payload and keeps the total count up to date. Pages are 1-based and always clamped to [1, lastPage]. By default a change of the query built by the previous extensions (filters, sorts, ...) between two reads brings the page back to 1 (PaginationExtensionOptions.resetPageOnQueryChange). Every event is raised only when the value actually changes (see PaginationExtensionEvents).

    OptionalreadItems

    • readItems?(
          payload: DataSourcePayload<TItem>,
          next: DataSourceReadNext<TItem>,
          signal?: AbortSignal,
      ): Promise<List<TItem>>

      Wraps the reading of the items. Read phase: the extension is a layer of the onion around the reader, next the layers inside it (the inner extensions, then the reader of the data source). The payload is the whole query, whatever the position of the extension.

      next may be called zero times (e.g. a cache hit: the reader is never invoked), once, with this or another payload, or several times (e.g. a payload split into several reads whose results are merged); every call runs the inner layers and the reader again, so the hooks and the events of the inner extensions may fire as many times. The result may be transformed before being returned, and flows outward: the outermost extension transforms last.

      Parameters

      • payload: DataSourcePayload<TItem>

        The whole query, built by the build phase.

      • next: DataSourceReadNext<TItem>

        Reads the items through the inner layers and the reader; the signal of the read is bound already.

      • Optionalsignal: AbortSignal

        Signal of the read, aborted when the read is superseded, for the reads of the extension itself.

      Returns Promise<List<TItem>>

      the items for the outer extension, or the data source.
