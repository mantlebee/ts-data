    Class DataSourceExtension<TItem, TEvents>Abstract

    Base class of the extensions shipped with the package: a Subscribable of ts-core with a cancellable, debounced *Change event. Subclasses call _scheduleChange after every mutation and raise their own event in _triggerChange. Custom extensions do not have to extend it: implementing IDataSourceExtension is enough.
